JAGM AI Clinic

Meet our two characters. They are both Claude, but they work in two different ways. Dr. Sage is Chat. He is a PhD-level consultant who sits across the table from you. He brainstorms, spars with you, checks your work, makes lists and explains anything. You stay in charge. You get answers and drafts that you copy out. Mr. Done is Cowork, as in "get it done". He opens your folders, builds decks, sheets and documents, repeats a task many times and can even run on a schedule. You get finished files in your folder. Rule of thumb: need an answer or a draft? Ask Dr. Sage. Need a finished file, or the same task many times? Give it to Mr. Done.

How to ask Claude well

Excellence in Prompting

The prompt formula

Role: You are a…

Expert in: You know a lot about…

Where: You work at JAGM, which…

Goal: Your goal is to…

Tools: Use these files…

Steps: First… then… ask me before…

Weak prompt

“Write a grant.”

Strong prompt

“You are a grant writer at Junior Achievement of Greater Miami. You know youth education funding well. Your goal is a clear, strong first draft for [funder name]. Use the files in this folder. Ask me questions before you start.”

JAGM AI Clinic · Always check before you send

You are here · Level 1

Excellence in prompting. Remember context? A prompt is your briefing for Dr. Sage. Most people type one short line, like "write a grant", and Claude has to guess everything. Instead, give a job description: the role, what it is an expert in, where it works, the goal, which files or tools to use, and the steps. Look at the weak and the strong prompt. Quick try, 3 minutes: take a task from your week and write it with this formula. Remember: Claude can sound sure and still be wrong. Always check before you send.

JAGM AI Clinic

Level 3

Three words you will hear a lot: skill, agent and workflow. A skill is a saved how-to: the rules and examples for one kind of task, written once and reused every time. In our story, it is the badge a Helper Agent wears. An agent is a helper that takes a goal, plans the steps, uses files and tools, and keeps working until it is done. Mr. Done is our lead agent, and the Helper Agents are his specialists. A workflow is the order the work happens in: who does what, and who hands work to whom. Example: Golf Invitational thank-you letters. Mr. Done opens the sponsor list. The Letter Agent drafts a letter for each sponsor using our letter-style skill. The File Naming Agent names every file with The JAGM Way skill. Mr. Done saves them and says: done, 20 letters in your folder. Next year, we run the same workflow again with one sentence. A skill is what you know. An agent is who does it. A workflow is the order it happens in.
