01

🎯 Purpose

JAGM generates photos and video at nearly every event, classroom visit, and program session. Without a shared process, that content ends up scattered across phones, group chats, and personal drives. This playbook gives every contributor one path from camera to official archive. It applies to all staff, volunteers, program leads, and contracted photographers who capture JAGM visuals for marketing, communications, or donor-facing use.

02

📋 Step-by-step

STEP 1 OF 6📣 Marketing Manager

📸 Capture

Who
Staff, volunteers, program leads; professional photographer on scheduled shoots
What
Take photos/video at the event, class visit, or program session
How
Everyday phone photos go to the JAGM_Visual_Share WhatsApp group same day; professional/DSLR shoots are delivered by the photographer to the Marketing Manager within 5 business days
Tip
Get one wide/room shot, one action shot, and one posed group shot at every event
STEP 2 OF 6 · DECISION POINT🛡️ Program / Event Lead

✅ Consent check (non-negotiable)

Who
Program or event lead on site
What
Confirm media-release status before any identifiable student appears in external-facing content
How
Check the partner school's/event's media release list before the event; if a student's status is unknown or declined, do not feature them as an identifiable subject in Flickr, social, or website use; favor wide/group shots over close-ups of unconfirmed individuals when unsure
This is the fork in the diagram above. Every asset passes through this one decision. Cleared → continues to Flickr and the official SharePoint archive. Unclear or declined → stops here, permanently, in the internal Google Photos library only — it never reaches Flickr or SharePoint's public-facing folders.
STEP 3 OF 6🗂️ Digital Marketing Manager

📥 Central intake

What
Every raw asset lands in one shared library before anything goes further
How
Upload raw files from WhatsApp and camera shoots to the shared JAGM Google Photos account within 5 business days
Output
Unedited raw library — single source of truth
STEP 4 OF 6🗂️ Digital Marketing Manager

🗃️ Organize

What
Sort raw uploads into one named album per event/program
How
Create an album using the naming convention below; share access with relevant team members and program leads
Output
Organized, shareable albums by event
STEP 5 OF 6📣 Marketing Manager

📤 External share

What
Curated, consent-cleared highlight sets for public use
How
Select best consent-cleared images from each album; upload to JAGM Flickr under the same naming convention
Used for
Website, newsletters, donor reports, social media, partner requests
STEP 6 OF 6🏛️ Operations Manager

🗄️ Official archive

What
Permanent, official system of record
How
Within 30 days of the event, copy the final curated set plus hero images to Marketing → Visual Archive on SharePoint, in a matching named subfolder
Output
The version that survives platform changes, account changes, and staff turnover
Why Ops
This copy is the official system of record, not a working or promotional library
03

👥 Roles & responsibilities

🙋 Field contributors

  • Capture and submit visuals promptly
  • Flag consent concerns at the point of capture

🛡️ Program / event lead

  • Confirm consent status on-site before submission

📣 Marketing Manager

  • Owns Capture coordination, incl. professional/DSLR delivery
  • Owns Flickr external share

🗂️ Digital Marketing Manager

  • Owns Google Photos central intake
  • Owns album organization

🏛️ Operations Manager

  • Owns the official SharePoint archive
04

🏷️ Naming convention

JAGM_Job_Shadow_Day_Mar12_26
ORG TAG
Always JAGM
EVENT NAME
Underscores between words — never spaces or hyphens
DATE — MONTH + DAY
3-letter month directly followed by 2-digit day (e.g. Mar12)
DATE — YEAR
2-digit year, separated by an underscore
JAGM_Job_Shadow_Day_Mar12_26
JAGM_BizTown_Spring_Apr02_26
05

🗄️ Retention & housekeeping

Google Photos
Indefinite
Working/raw library, kept as backup — not the official archive
Flickr
Current + 2 years
Public galleries; older sets archived, not deleted
SharePoint
Permanent
Official archive — nothing deleted without Marketing Director sign-off

Marketing reviews albums quarterly for consent flags, naming consistency, and SharePoint sync gaps.

06

✅ Quick checklists

🙋 Field contributors

  • Phone photos shared to WhatsApp the same day
  • Professional/DSLR files delivered to the Marketing Manager within 5 business days
  • Consent status checked for any identifiable students
  • Event name and date included when submitting

📣 Marketing Manager

  • Professional/DSLR files received within 5 business days
  • Consent-cleared highlights selected and posted to Flickr

🗂️ Digital Marketing Manager

  • Uploaded to Google Photos within 5 business days
  • Album created and named correctly

🏛️ Operations Manager

  • Final set archived to SharePoint within 30 days
