01 · Purpose

JAGM generates photos and video at nearly every event, classroom visit, and program session. Without a shared process, that content ends up scattered across phones, group chats, and personal drives — hard to find and unsafe to reuse. This playbook gives every contributor one path from camera to official archive.

It applies to all staff, volunteers, program leads, and contracted photographers who capture JAGM visuals for marketing, communications, or donor-facing use.

03 · Step-by-step

01

Capture

Owner
Marketing Manager
Who
Staff, volunteers, program leads; professional photographer on scheduled shoots
What
Take photos/video at the event, class visit, or program session
How
  • Everyday phone photos → post directly to the JAGM_Visual_Share WhatsApp group
  • Professional/DSLR shoots → photographer delivers files to the Marketing Manager within 5 business days
Tip
Get one wide/room shot, one action shot, and one posed group shot at every event — covers most marketing needs.
02

Consent check

Who
Program or event lead on site
What
Confirm media-release status before any identifiable student appears in external-facing content
How
  • Check the partner school's or event's media release list before the event (usually collected annually)
  • If a student's status is unknown or declined, do not feature them as an identifiable subject in Flickr, social, or website use
  • When unsure, favor wide or group shots over close-ups of unconfirmed individuals
Non-negotiable step

This protects JAGM and our school partners. If consent can't be confirmed, the image stays in the internal Google Photos library only — it does not move to Flickr or SharePoint's public-facing folders.

03

Central intake — Google Photos

Owner
Digital Marketing Manager
What
Every raw asset lands in one shared library before anything goes further
How
Upload raw files from WhatsApp and camera shoots to the shared JAGM Google Photos account within 5 business days of the event
Output
Unedited raw library — the single source of truth for what was captured
04

Organize — Google Albums

Owner
Digital Marketing Manager
What
Sort raw uploads into one named album per event or program
How
Create an album using the standard naming convention (see Section 05) and share access with relevant team members and program leads
Output
Organized, shareable albums by event
05

External share — Flickr

Owner
Marketing Manager
What
Curated, consent-cleared highlight sets for public use
How
Select the best consent-cleared images from each album; upload to the JAGM Flickr account under the same naming convention
Used for
Website, newsletters, donor reports, social media, partner requests
06

Official archive — SharePoint

Owner
Operations Manager
What
Permanent, official system of record
How
Within 30 days of the event, copy the final curated set plus any hero images to Marketing → Visual Archive on SharePoint, in a matching named subfolder
Output
The version that survives platform changes, account changes, and staff turnover
Why
Kept with Operations rather than Marketing because this copy is the official system of record, not a working or promotional library

05 · Naming convention

Every album, Flickr set, and SharePoint subfolder for the same event uses one name, so it's findable everywhere:

JAGM_[Event_Name]_[Mon][DD]_[YY] e.g. JAGM_Job_Shadow_Day_Mar12_26 JAGM_BizTown_Spring_Apr02_26

Use underscores, not spaces or hyphens, between words in the event name. Date is a 3-letter month directly followed by a 2-digit day (e.g. Mar12), then an underscore and 2-digit year.
